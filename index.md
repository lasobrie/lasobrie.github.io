Hello! I am **Léon Sobrie**, Postdoctoral Researcher at the System Performance Lab in Grado Department of Industrial & Systems Engineering at Virginia Tech (VT). 

My research focuses on **human-in-the-loop decision support in safety-critical systems**, where I combine predictive analytics, machine learning, causal inference, and systems thinking. Before joining VT, I earned my PhD in Business Economics from Ghent University, where I researched explainable AI and decision support for real-time control room operations. 

I focus on **designing intelligent, human-centered systems that support decision-making in complex, high-stakes environments**. My expertise builds on both quantitative methods (like deep learning and causal machine learning) and qualitative methods (like field observations and interviews) to develop solutions that are effective and explainable.

Always happy to connect and exchange ideas!
