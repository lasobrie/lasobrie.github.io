Hello! I'm **Léon Sobrie**, an AI & Data Consultant at [delaware BeLux](https://www.delaware.pro/en-be), where I focus on the technical execution of AI solutions using development best practices. I also get involved in identifying meaningful AI use cases with clients and supporting presales, bridging the gap between what AI can do and what businesses actually need.

My background is in research: I earned my PhD in Business Economics from Ghent University, where I explored explainable AI and decision support for real-time control room operations. I then worked as a Postdoctoral Researcher at Virginia Tech's System Performance Lab, combining AI, systems thinking, and human-centered design for safety-critical systems. I remain voluntarily affiliated with both Ghent University and Virginia Tech, keeping one foot in the research world.

These days, I'm energized by turning AI research into real-world impact by designing and shipping solutions that are both technically solid and genuinely useful to the people who use them.

Always happy to connect and exchange ideas!
